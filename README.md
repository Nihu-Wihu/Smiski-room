# Smiski-room
**Made for Playhouse hackclub! And my first attempt at CAD and 3D printing!**

I started by getting an account on Onshape! And then I immediately started figuring out how to do he dimensions of the room by following the guide in Playhouse...

Most of the furniture is smooth with rounded corners and a soft pastel color scheme with lots of pastel pink and green accents. 
**On the Upper Level:**
- A loft bed with a built-in bookshelf
- A small study desk and stool tucked underneath the bed.
- A round armchair on a circular green rug
- Wall decorations including pictures, a small window, and a coat rack.

**On the Lower Level:**
- A set of stairs and a railing connecting the two floors
- A  two-seater sofa with round pillows.
- A small coffee table on another circular green rug.
- A wall-mounted TV above
